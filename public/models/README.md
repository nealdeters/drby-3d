Source: 3dassets.dev pack **Horse Stables and Equestrian Yard**

- `horse-stand.glb` — Riding Horse Standing (Bay)

- `horse-trot.glb` — Riding Horse Trotting (Bay)
  https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/23751/v1/model.glb

Same topology (7,068 verts). DRBY morphs stand→trot on the stride and
recolors the `bay` material per horse. Static posed figures; no skin clip.
